Need Answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and processes.

What is the difference between Hand Polish and PU Finish?
Hand Polish is a traditional technique applied by hand — it gives wood a warm, natural, satin sheen and is ideal for antique and classic furniture. PU (Polyurethane) Finish is a modern sprayed coating that forms a hard protective layer, highly resistant to scratches, water, and heat, available in matte, satin, and gloss. We help you choose based on your furniture type and daily usage.
How long does it take to polish a piece of furniture?
It depends on the size, condition, and chosen finish. A single chair or table typically takes 1–2 days, while a complete bed or wardrobe set may take 3–7 days. Antique restoration can take longer. We always share a clear timeline with your quote.
Do you provide on-site (home) polishing service?
Yes. For larger items like wardrobes, beds, doors, and panelling we polish on-site across Kolkata with proper dust protection. Delicate or highly detailed work is best done at our Kasba workshop — we can arrange pickup and delivery.
Which areas of Kolkata do you serve?
Our workshop is in Kasba, and we serve all of Kolkata — including South Kolkata (Ballygunge, Gariahat, Tollygunge, Behala), Central and North Kolkata, Salt Lake, New Town, and nearby areas like Howrah. Call us to confirm service in your locality.
How much does furniture polishing cost?
Cost depends on the finish (Hand Polish, PU, Polycoat), the size of the item, and its current condition. We provide a free inspection and a transparent, no-obligation quote — just call or WhatsApp us with a photo of your furniture.
Can you restore old or damaged furniture?
Absolutely — restoration is our specialty. We revive dull, scratched, water-stained, or peeled finishes, repair minor damage, and bring back the original beauty of your cherished pieces, including antique and heirloom furniture.
